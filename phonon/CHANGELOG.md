# Changelog

## 0.4.0

- Runs the Phonon 0.4.0 server.
- No model needed: English uses Phonon 31m, and French, German, Spanish and
  Portuguese use their Phonon 40m model (gated: set a Hugging Face token).
- The voices of the Phonon voice catalog in your language are listed in Home
  Assistant.
- The **Model** option takes `owner/name@commit` for a pinned version. The
  **Revision** option is gone.

## 0.1.0

- First release: Phonon over the Wyoming protocol, with streaming.
